Hail Mary redux

Consider the following situation:

A society composed of both humans and an alien species can only interact with one another (both within and across groups) by sending emails (they speak a common language). Model this system as the various agents learn to cooperate with one another while avoiding any pitfalls. Without references to previous models:

  • Model, using whatever techniques you wish, the above scenario.

    • Explicitly state your model and key assumptions.
    • Summarize key results.
    • Suggest some potentially interesting future directions and questions for the model.
    • Suggest a few applications of your model to the social sciences.
